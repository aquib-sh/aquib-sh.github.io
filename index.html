Hi, I'm Aquib Shaikh

Software Engineer & Linux Enthusiast

I'm a software engineer working at Mirae Asset Capital Markets, where I get to work with SWIFT protocol systems. Think of it like UPI or PayPal, but for financial institutions that move millions of dollars globally. The cool part is that SWIFT doesn't actually transfer money - it just transmits instructions between banks.

I've done my fair share of different things - 3 years of freelancing where I completed around 200 projects, multiple internships, and even worked as a founding engineer at a startup where I built the entire product from scratch. Each experience was unique and taught me something valuable.

While I work with C# (.NET Core) in my current role, Python is where my heart lies. That's where I built my programming foundation, and during my freelancing journey from 2020-2023, I did tons of projects in Python - web scraping, automation, scripting, ETL, GUI/CLI tools, you name it. The best part was getting to work with people from dozens of countries - it really broadened my perspective.

And Linux... I'm a total nerd when it comes to it. I can't count how many times my Linux tinkering has helped me in my work. Whether it's server deployments or understanding how computers work at a deeper level, Linux has changed how I think about technology. My love for programming and Linux go hand in hand - they're both about understanding and building complex systems.
