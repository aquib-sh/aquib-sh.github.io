Hey, I’m Aquib — a backend engineer who enjoys building things that scale, optimizing systems, and occasionally getting lost in complex open source systems and Linux rabbit hole.

Right now, I’m working at Oneture Technologies, where I’m helping modernize fintech systems with .NET Core and SWIFT messaging — think legacy banking, but faster, cleaner, and less painful. Before this, I was the lone backend dev at a startup, building a full-blown data integration platform around Tally ERP. That meant working across .NET, Python, Go, MongoDB, and PostgreSQL, designing microservices, API gateways, PDF generators — the works.

I didn’t come up the “traditional” way either. I started off as a freelancer during the COVID days, delivered over 150 projects to clients in 23+ countries — everything from automation scripts to full-blown apps. Somewhere along the way, I also did a bit of cybersecurity with Maharashtra Cyber (reverse engineering sketchy Android apps was… interesting), and even a brief stint in Flutter dev just to see what the hype was about.

These days, I’m mostly into backend architecture, microservices, system design, performance tuning, and occasionally some DSA and low-level tinkering. I’m also into open-source, Neovim configs, and generally making my dev setup feel like home.

Outside tech, you’ll catch me hiking in the Sahyadris, I also have a [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@aquibshaikh8611), Where I post raw, unfiltered (mostly) trek clips. One of my favs: [Sondai Fort Trek](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QFN0jWkZOlc&list=PLNbfLpF69aDp1Qy1oeA5nHPKyPpsVNjld&index=8). 

This site will eventually host some tech blogs, book recs, and dev logs. Until then, feel free to explore, or hit me up if you wanna geek out over backend systems, reverse proxies, or even just Neovim plugin drama.

Stay curious.
